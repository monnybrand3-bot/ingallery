Common Questions

Everything you need to know about InGallery billing.

Can I change my plan at any time? arrow_outward

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any point. If you upgrade, the new features will be available immediately and charges will be prorated.

What happens if I exceed my storage? arrow_outward

We'll notify you when you reach 90% capacity. If you exceed it, you'll still be able to share existing galleries, but new uploads will be paused until you upgrade.

Are there any hidden transaction fees? arrow_outward

InGallery does not charge commission on your sales. Standard Stripe processing fees apply if you use our integrated client print store.

How secure is my data? arrow_outward

We use AES-256 encryption for all stored files and high-availability server clusters to ensure your portfolio is always safe and accessible.
